About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web application designed to help you organize thoughts, manage daily tasks, and capture quick ideas effortlessly.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Click on the text area to start typing your note.
  2. Assign a label or category to keep your notes organized.
  3. Click the save button to persist your notes locally.

Features

  • Fast local storage integration
  • Clean, distraction-free interface
  • Customizable note categories
